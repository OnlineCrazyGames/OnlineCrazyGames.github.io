Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information Online Crazy Games ("we", "us") handles when you use our website.

Information we collect

You don't need an account to play, and we don't ask for personal details to use the site. If you email us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your name and email address, and use it only to reply.

Data stored in your browser

Games and the site may save small amounts of data in your browser (local storage), such as cached game lists and your game scores. This data stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Third-party services

  • Google Fonts is used to load fonts, which may involve your browser contacting Google's servers.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this site and may log standard technical data such as IP addresses.
  • Advertising. We show ads provided by a third-party advertising network. Ad providers may use cookies or similar technologies and may collect information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you view, in order to show and measure ads. We do not control these providers. Please review their own privacy policies, and you can limit ad tracking through your browser settings or an ad blocker.

Children's privacy

Our site is suitable for general audiences and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can clear stored data and block cookies through your browser at any time. To ask about data we may hold, contact us using the details below.
